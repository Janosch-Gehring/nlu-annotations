# Introduction to the Word Meaning Plausibility + Connection Task

## Overview

This is a joint study. That means that you will have to do two tasks in succession:

1) **Word Meaning Plausibility Task**: Judging the plausibility of a word's meaning in a short text given a specified category
2) **Term Connection Task**: Puzzles where you get 5 words and connect each to one of 5 categories (ignoring unfamiliar words)

There is a qualification test for each one too. As such, your total workload will be:

- Clearing the Word Meaning Plausibility Qualification Test (4 examples)
- Clearing the Term Connection Task Qualification Test (Doing 1 5x5 puzzle)
- Finishing the Word Meaning Plausibility Main Study (10 more texts)
- Finishing the Term Connection Task (Doing about 6 5x5 puzzles)

So there are about 20 samples in total. **Please go through the tasks in the order of the left sidebar.**
